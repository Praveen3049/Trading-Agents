### 2. Core Strategy Concept
* **1 AM CRT:** Trading a single 4-Hour (4H) candle based on its Open-High-Low-Close (OHLC / OLHC) profile.
* **Core Philosophy:** Moving away from rigid, lagging "pattern matching" and focusing entirely on **Time, Location, and Market Context (Logic)**.
* **Instrument Specifics:**
  * **Forex (EUR/USD, etc.):** The key 4H candle starts at **1:00 AM** and ends at **5:00 AM** (spanning the London Killzone).
  * **Indices:** The key 4H candle starts at 2:00 AM.
* **Key Idea:** Capturing engineered liquidity purges (Turtle Soup) during key time windows using higher timeframe (HTF) premium/discount arrays and lower timeframe (LTF) order blocks / Model-1 structural shifts.

### 3. Timeframes, Session Timings & Key Ranges
* **Daily Timeframe:** Establishes the daily candle sequence and macro range:

* **Intermediate Timeframe (4H):** Used for market context, narrative confirmation, and tapping into HTF premium/discount arrays (e.g., HTF PD array / weekly highs/lows).

  * **3:00 AM Window:** The critical execution window (3:00 AM – 4:00 AM) where the liquidity purge (Turtle Soup) occurs.

### 4. Structural Setup Mechanics
1. **HTF Context (Daily/4H):** Price purges a key weekly high (or session high) and taps into a higher timeframe premium PD array.
2. **Liquidity Engineering:** Look for obvious pre-drawn liquidity (e.g., 9:00 PM range high/low or previous session swing points).
3. **The Purge (Turtle Soup):** During the 1:00 AM CRT candle (specifically inside the 3:00 AM – 4:00 AM window), price sweeps the prior high (9:00 PM range high) to grab buy-stop liquidity.
4. **Displacement / Market Structure Shift (MSS):** Following the liquidity purge, price rapidly shifts state (Model-1 / change in state of delivery) by breaking consecutive lower candles and forming an **Order Block (OB)** or Fair Value Gap (FVG).

---

### 5. Entry Triggers & Model Confirmations
* **Model-1 / OB Entry:** When the Model-1 candle (the swing-purging candle) is too small to use directly as an entry trigger, drop down to the order block left behind by the consecutive candle close below structural levels.
* **Execution Trigger:** Enter short directly on the retest/mitigation of the M15 Order Block formed immediately after the purge of the 9:00 PM high during the 3:00 AM window.

---

### 6. Invalidation & Stop Loss Rules
* **Stop Loss Placement:** Placed just above the swing high (the exact candle high that executed the "turtle soup" / purge of liquidity).
* **Invalidation Level:** Any structural breach closing above the engineered turtle soup high.

---

### 7. Target / Draw on Liquidity (DOL) & Risk Management
* **Take Profit / DOL:** Mechanically targeted at **3R (3-to-1 Risk-to-Reward ratio)** or extended toward the opposite side liquidity pool (e.g., the 2nd candle low / opposite session low).
* **Management Philosophy:** Mechanical execution. Once 3R is achieved, the trade is closed. Do not hold greedily for extended moves if macro risk parameters are met.

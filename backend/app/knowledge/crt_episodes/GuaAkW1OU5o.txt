### 3. Timeframes, Session Timings & Key Ranges

* **Execution Timeframe:** 4-Hour (4H) and 1-Hour (1H) for structural confirmation and entries.

### 4. Structural Setup Mechanics
1. **HTF Liquidity Sweep (Turtle Soup):** Price takes out a major high or low (e.g., Monthly/Daily old high) and immediately closes back inside the range. This confirms a valid Turtle Soup (Phase 1).
2. **Candle Range Definition (CRH / CRL):** The specific range of the candle that executed the sweep becomes the reference zone.
3. **LTF Order Block / FVG Alignment:** Within the 4H timeframe, look for 4H Fair Value Gaps or Order Blocks/Propulsion Blocks aligned inside the HTF candle range.
4. **Model-1 Shift:** A displacement on lower timeframes that breaks a short-term swing structure, confirming the shift in algorithmic delivery.

### 5. Entry Triggers & Model Confirmations
* **Rule of Patience:** Do not jump into a Turtle Soup blind. Wait for **confirmation**—price must sweep the liquidity level and close back inside.
* **The 4H / 1H Tap:** Wait for LTF price action during specific killzones (Asia/London open) to tap into the 4H FVG or Order Block (Breaker/Kiss of Death).
* **Model-1 Trigger:** Look for a 4H/1H market structure shift (MSS) closing below/above the manipulation candle that purged the liquidity.

### 6. Invalidation & Stop Loss Rules
* **Stop Loss Placement:** Placed safely beyond the extreme wick of the HTF/LTF turtle soup manipulation high/low or above the 4H order block boundary.
* **Time-Based Invalidation:** If price fails to deliver toward the intended DOL within the expected session window (e.g., failing to expand post-Tuesday manipulation), the setup is invalidated.

### 7. Target / Draw on Liquidity (DOL) & Risk Management
* **Targets:** Internal range liquidity, opposing 4H/Daily FVG arrays, or the opposite end of the HTF candle range (e.g., from the high sweep down to the candle's low or midpoint equilibrium).
* **Risk Discipline:** Trading is 90% patience and waiting. Only execute when HTF candle phases align perfectly with LTF range execution models.

### 2. Core Strategy Concept
* **Model-1 Entry Model:** A systematic, high-probability confirmation entry model that bridges Higher Timeframe (HTF) order flow with Lower Timeframe (LTF) execution. 
* It requires zero guessing: price must first purge external liquidity (taking out a candle range high/low, old high/low, or CRT high/low) **AND** fill an existing Fair Value Gap (FVG) / rebalance an imbalance. 
* Once liquidity is taken and the model candle is formed/engulfed, execution is derived on the LTF using precise candle-close criteria.

---

### 3. Timeframes, Session Timings & Key Ranges
* **HTF to LTF Alignment Framework:**
  * **Monthly CRT** $\rightarrow$ Execute on **Daily** LTF
  * **Weekly CRT** $\rightarrow$ Execute on **4H** LTF
  * **Daily CRT** $\rightarrow$ Execute on **1H** LTF
  * **4H CRT** $\rightarrow$ Execute on **15M** LTF
  * **1H CRT** $\rightarrow$ Execute on **5M** LTF
* **Timing Discipline:** Entries should strictly align with key time windows (killzones, specific refined sessions) to avoid random market execution.

---

### 4. Structural Setup Mechanics
* **Purge Phase:** Price runs an explicit HTF or LTF key level (candle range high/low or old swing high/low) to trigger trapped liquidity and stops.
* **Imbalance / FVG Rebalancing:** Concurrently or sequentially, price fills an imbalance (FVG) or tests an existing order block.
* **Model-1 Dual Condition (The "Both-Job" Candle):** The optimal setup occurs when a single candle (or immediate sequence) **both** purges liquidity **and** rebalances an FVG/order block simultaneously.

---

### 5. Entry Triggers & Model Confirmations
* **Engulfing Confirmation:** Wait for price to close below/above the purging candle in the intended direction.
* **Retest Confirmation:** Price must return to retest the exact model candle (respecting it) before continuation.
* **Opening Price vs. Wick Selection Rules:**
  * **Use Opening Price:** When the model candle is large, strong, and beefy, use its open price for a tighter, more reliable entry.
  * **Use High/Low (Wick):** When the candle has a small body and a long wick (where the wick carries more structural weight), or when the close breaks beyond the high/low, use the wick level for entry.

---

### 6. Invalidation & Stop Loss Rules
* **Stop Loss Placement:** Placed safely above/below the model candle’s high/low or the immediate swing high/low structure.
* **Invalidation:** If price fails to retrace and impulses aggressively away without a retest, **miss the trade.** Missing a trade costs nothing; forcing a trade damages capital and psychology.

---

### 7. Target / Draw on Liquidity (DOL) & Risk Management
* **Targets:** Aim for the opposite end of the HTF/LTF CRT range, or take partial profits at the 50% equilibrium level of the range.
* **Risk-to-Reward:** Model-1 provides asymmetric R:R (high reward relative to tight, defined risk).

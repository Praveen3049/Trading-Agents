### 2. Core Strategy Concept
The lecture teaches the **HTF > ITF > LTF Syncing Process** using Candle Range Theory (CRT) combined with institutional Smart Money Concepts (SMC). The core premise is that a trader must build a clear, logical trading narrative by descending from Higher Timeframes (HTF - Daily/4H) to Intermediate Timeframes (ITF - 4H/1H/Killzones) down to Lower Timeframe execution (LTF - 15m/5m). 

* **HTF:** Understand where market order flow is directed, establish current market bias, and identify the directional Draw on Liquidity (DOL).
* **ITF:** Establish time and price narratives (Killzones, PO3, Dealing Ranges, Turtle soups, CRT context) and refine key levels.
* **LTF:** Execute based on timing (Killzones), model-based entries (e.g., Model-1 Order Block/Turtle Soup), protected swing stop loss placement, and predefined CRT risk-to-reward targets.

### 3. Timeframes, Session Timings & Key Ranges
* **HTF (Higher Timeframe):** Daily Chart (Order Flow, Bias, DOL, Key Levels like FVG / Order Blocks).
* **ITF (Intermediate Timeframe):** 4-Hour / Hourly (Context, narrative, refinement of key levels, CRT range integration).
* **LTF (Lower Timeframe):** 15-Minute / Lower (Execution, Model-1 entry, structural defense).
* **Session Timings (Killzones - UTC-5 / UTC-4 adjusted):**
  * **London Killzone:** 02:00 AM – 05:00 AM (UTC-5/4)
  * **New York Killzone:** 07:00 AM – 10:00 / 11:00 AM (UTC-5/4)
  * *Rule:* Ignore local country time; set charts explicitly to UTC-5 or UTC-4 to match institutional algorithmic killzones.

---

### 4. Structural Setup Mechanics
* **HTF Order Flow:** Identifying the prevailing direction of price delivery (bullish = rejecting below lows and breaking above highs; bearish = rejecting above highs and breaking below lows).
* **HTF Bias & DOL (Draw on Liquidity):** Determining if candles close above/inside/below previous candles to establish directional bias and target liquidity pools.
* **ITF Context & Narrative:** Using time-based narratives (Killzones, OHLC/OLHC profiles) and price-based narratives (PO3, dealing ranges, turtle soups, CRT framework).
* **LTF Setup (Turtle Soup & Displacement):** 
  * Price sweeps (purges) a lower-timeframe swing low/high (Turtle Soup).
  * Price reacts off a refined ITF/HTF key level (Order Block or Fair Value Gap - FVG).
  * Market creates a displacement / Market Structure Shift (MSS) closing above/below key structural candles.

---

### 5. Entry Triggers & Model Confirmations
* **Model-1 Entry (Order Block / CRT Model):** 
  * 1. Price purges a liquidity level (swing low/high) during an institutional Killzone.
  * 2. Price taps a refined key level (HTF/ITF Order Block or FVG).
  * 3. LTF candle closes back across the structural pivot / range (CRT candle close confirmation).
  * 4. Entry is taken on the open/retest of the confirming candle or model-1 specification.

---

### 6. Invalidation & Stop Loss Rules
* **Stop Loss Placement:** Always placed beyond the **Protected Swing High / Low** (the structural swing point that created the Model-1 entry setup). 
* **Invalidation:** If price breaks the protected swing high/low, the narrative/trade idea is invalidated. Unhedged/blind stops are prohibited; structural defense is mandatory.

---

### 7. Target / Draw on Liquidity (DOL) & Risk Management
* **Targets (TP):** 
  * 50% of the CRT range
  * CRT High / Low (depending on trade direction)
  * Major Higher Timeframe liquidity pools (DOL) for runners (with partial profits taken beforehand).
* **Risk-to-Reward Ratio (RR):** Minimum **1:2 to 1:3 RR** per execution to ensure a steadily growing equity curve even with modest win rates.

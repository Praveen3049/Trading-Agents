### 4. Structural Setup Mechanics (Bullish OHLC Example)
1. **Open:** Candle opens.
2. **Liquidity Build-up / Turtle Soup (TS):** Price sweeps a short-term low (generates liquidity, then triggers a Turtle Soup on the HTF wick/low). *Real displacement must follow immediately—no consolidation.*
3. **Displacement / Expansion:** Aggressive price movement upward, breaking the short-term high to show institutional intent.
4. **Market Structure Shift (MSS) / FVG Fill:** Price breaks structure, fills Fair Value Gaps, and confirms a shift in momentum.
5. **Breaker Block Phase:** Price pulls back to retest the Breaker Block zone combined with the Optimal Trade Entry (OTE / Fibonacci 0.618–0.786 levels).

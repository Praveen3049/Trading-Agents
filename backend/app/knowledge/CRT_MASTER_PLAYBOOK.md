## 1. THE FOUNDATIONAL EQUATION (Episode 1)
$$\text{Liquidity} + \text{Time} + \text{Price} = \text{Time-Based Ranges}$$

Rather than subjective chart patterns or lagging retail indicators, **every candlestick on any timeframe is an objective, time-based range** possessing:
* **$\text{CRH}$ (Candle Range High)** = Buy-Side Liquidity Pool.
* **$\text{CRL}$ (Candle Range Low)** = Sell-Side Liquidity Pool.
* **$50\%$ Equilibrium** = Balance point between Premium and Discount.

### The 3 Canonical CRT Types:
1. **Three-Candle CRT (Core Engine)**:
   * **Candle 1**: Accumulation (Sets the benchmark range).
   * **Candle 2**: Manipulation (Turtle Soup sweep into an HTF Key Level).
   * **Candle 3**: Distribution (Expands toward the opposing range boundary).
2. **Two-Candle CRT (High Volatility / News)**:
   * **Candle 1**: Establishes range.
   * **Candle 2**: Performs dual duty—purges liquidity *and* delivers full expansion in the same candle.
3. **Multiple-Candle CRT (Inside / Outside Bars)**:
   * **Inside Bars**: Consolidation inside mother candle until liquidity is raided.
   * **Outside Bars**: Engulfing raid followed by multi-candle expansion.

## 2. MODEL-1: LOWER TIMEFRAME EXECUTION ENGINE (Episode 2)
Model-1 is the non-negotiable entry confirmation that bridges Higher Timeframe context to Lower Timeframe precision.

### Timeframe Mapping Hierarchy:
* **Monthly CRT** $\longrightarrow$ **Daily (D1) LTF Execution**
* **Weekly CRT** $\longrightarrow$ **4-Hour (H4) LTF Execution**
* **Daily CRT** $\longrightarrow$ **1-Hour (H1) LTF Execution**
* **4-Hour CRT** $\longrightarrow$ **15-Minute (M15) LTF Execution** *(Core Desk Anchor)*
* **1-Hour CRT** $\longrightarrow$ **5-Minute (M5) LTF Execution** *(Scalping)*

### The Dual-Condition Rule ("Both-Job" Candle):
A high-probability Model-1 candle must execute two simultaneous tasks:
1. **Job 1**: Purge external liquidity (taking out CRH/CRL or an old high/low).
2. **Job 2**: Tap and rebalance an unmitigated **Fair Value Gap (FVG)** or Order Block.

### Entry Limit Selection Rules:
* **Large Beefy Candle Body**: Place entry limit at the **Open** of the model candle for maximum precision and tighter risk.
* **Long Wick / Small Body**: Place entry limit at the **Wick Extreme (High/Low)** because the wick carries the true structural liquidity.
* *Discipline Rule*: Never chase. If price impulses away without a retest, stand aside.

## 4. THE 5-PHASE LIFECYCLE (TSQ — THE SEQUENCE) (Episode 4)
Between a liquidity purge and target delivery, price travels through 5 sequential phases:
$$\text{Phase 1 (Turtle Soup)} \longrightarrow \text{Phase 2 (Model-1)} \longrightarrow \text{Phase 3 (Breaker Block)} \longrightarrow \text{Phase 4 (OTE)} \longrightarrow \text{Phase 5 (DOL Delivery)}$$

1. **Phase 1: Turtle Soup (TS)**: Smart money drives price beyond CRH/CRL to trap retail breakout orders.
2. **Phase 2: Model-1 Engine**: Immediate rejection candle closing back across the purged level (Primary Entry).
3. **Phase 3: Breaker Block (BRK)**: Market Structure Shift (MSS) breaks internal pivots, turning the failed swing into an institutional retest level.
4. **Phase 4: Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)**: Retracement into the **61.8% to 78.6% Fibonacci pocket** aligned with an FVG (Secondary Re-Fueling Entry).
5. **Phase 5: Final Delivery (DOL)**: Terminal expansion sweeping the opposing range boundary.

## 5. THE 4-QUARTER CANDLE LIFECYCLE (Episode 5)
Every single candlestick develops in 4 fractal quarters:
* **Quarter 1 (First 25% of Time)**: Range / Liquidity build-up.
* **Quarter 2 (Second 25% of Time)**: Manipulation (Turtle Soup) + rapid displacement away.
* **Quarter 3 (Third 25% of Time)**: **Kiss of Death (KOD)** — deep retracement into Breaker Block + OTE.
* **Quarter 4 (Final 25% of Time)**: Terminal expansion & candle capping.

> **The Golden Law of Confirmation:**  
> *"Closes tell the truth, wicks lie, closes confirm."*  
> If price raids liquidity and consolidates/lingers instead of rapidly displacing within 1–2 candles, **invalidate the setup immediately**.
